Sumi-e ink brush painting on aged cream washi paper. A large enso circle drawn with one dry
black brush stroke, the circle left open with a wide wedge-shaped gap like a hungry mouth
facing right. Four small ink-wash ghost creatures with wavy lower edges and two dot eyes each,
pressing in from the left side toward the open circle; two of them tinted with cinnabar red
wash, two painted in diluted indigo. Loose calligraphic strokes, dry-brush streaks, ink bleed,
a few accidental ink splatters, a small red hanko seal stamp in the lower right corner.
Minimalist asymmetric composition, generous empty paper around the subjects, muted black and
grey washes with red accents, hand painted, elegant, no text, no lettering, no border.
