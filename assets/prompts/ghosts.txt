Four separate sumi-e ink brush ghosts arranged in one horizontal row on plain white paper,
evenly spaced with wide empty gaps between them, all the same size. Each ghost is a rounded
hooded body with a wavy three-lobed lower edge and two small black dot eyes, painted in loose
black ink wash with dry-brush edges and soft ink bleed. The first ghost tinted with cinnabar
red wash, the second with indigo blue wash, the third with ochre brown wash, the fourth with
moss green wash. Minimal, calligraphic, hand painted, no background scenery, no text, no
lettering, no border, no shadow.
